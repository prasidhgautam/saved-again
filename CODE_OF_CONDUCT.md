# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participate with empathy, patience and respect. Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Give specific technical feedback without insults, harassment, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing or discrimination. Do not publish another person’s private data or real SavedAgain library content.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues, pull requests and contributions that violate this standard, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Avoid putting sensitive evidence in a public report. Use the repository’s private vulnerability-reporting channel for security matters and GitHub’s report/block tools for urgent platform abuse.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is representing the project. Enforcement decisions are made by the repository maintainers and may be reconsidered when new context is available.
